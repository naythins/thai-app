Cheat sheet

Numbers

  • ★ A final 1 in a number becomes èt → 11 = sìp-èt
  • ★ 20 = yîi-sìp (never sǎawng-sìp)
  • ★ 10 on its own = sìp

Verbs

  • ★ A Thai verb is never conjugated: one form for I / you / he, past or future
  • ★ No infinitive either: ไป bpai = to go / I go / he went
  • ★ Verbs stack directly → yàak gin (to want to eat), dtâwng bpai (to have to go)

Not shown on the avatar

  • ★ ขา khǎa covers the whole leg; เท้า tháo is the foot itself
  • ★ หัวใจ hǔa-jai (heart) is built on หัว hǔa (head)

Colours

  • ★ สี sǐi = colour; say it before the colour name → sǐi daeng (red)
  • ★ The colour comes after the thing → sûea sǐi daeng (a red shirt)
  • ★ เข้ม khêm = dark, อ่อน àawn = light, placed after the colour

Basic sentences

  • ★ จะ jà before the verb = future → phǒm jà bpai (I’ll go)
  • ★ กำลัง gam-lang before the verb = in the middle of doing
  • ★ แล้ว láew after the verb = already done
  • ★ ไม่ mâi before the verb = negation; no conjugation
